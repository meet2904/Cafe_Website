Artisanal Provenance & Ethos

The Art of Slow Extraction & Daily Craft

Founded in 2018, Beakk and Brew bridges the disciplined alchemy of third-wave Nordic roasting with the delicate traditions of classic French viennoiserie.

filter_vintage

High-Altitude Ethics

We deal exclusively with smallholder cooperatives across Huehuetenango, Sidama, and Nariño, paying an average of 45% above Fair Trade baselines. Every green bean is grown over 1,800 meters, ensuring dense bean density and vibrant natural sweetness.

Direct Trade Audited EST. 2018
fireplace

Measured Heat Profiles

Our master roasters roast in micro-batches of 12kg inside a customized vintage Giesen drum. Using sensory intuition and continuous airflow mapping, we preserve delicate florals, crisp stone fruit acids, and rich caramelized cacao undertones.

Nordic-Light Spectrum BATCH ROASTED
bakery_dining

Lamination & Ferment

Each croissant undergoes a disciplined 72-hour cold fermentation and folding cycle. We use 84% butterfat AOP Charentes-Poitou dry butter, yielding an impossibly gossamer honeycomb crumb that shatters upon first bite.

72-Hour Fermentation 100% HOUSE-MADE

"Coffee is not merely an extraction of soluble solids—it is a daily communion between soil, flame, and stillness."

— Éléonore Vance, Head Roaster & Co-Founder
